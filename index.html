About the Lessons

All lessons are delivered online via Zoom and last 45 minutes. The fee per lesson is £30. Lessons are tailored to your child's individual needs, working systematically through their GCSE curriculum with a focus on genuine understanding rather than just exam technique.

Initial Assessment Period

The first two lessons are used to assess your child's strengths, areas for development, and learning style, and to ensure that Mr Nathan's approach is the right fit for your child. If after those first two sessions we both feel it's a good match, we just continue. There's no pressure either way — getting the right fit is what matters most.

How We Work Together

Terms & Conditions — 45-Minute Lessons

Payment

Please pay £30 by bank transfer at least 24 hours before each lesson, using the bank details further down this page. That way your child's slot is confirmed. If a payment hasn't arrived by then, we'll simply move the lesson to another time.

Getting Online

All your child needs is a steady internet connection and a device with a working camera and microphone. A quick check before the first lesson is a good idea.

Homework

I set a little homework after each lesson. I ask that your child does it each week, ideally soon after the lesson while it's still fresh. It doesn't need to be perfect: an honest try is all I ask. It shows me what has gone in and what we need to look at again, and it helps your child see their own progress.

Working Together

Tuition works best as a team effort between your child, you and me, always with your child's progress and wellbeing at heart.

Communication

All correspondence is by email, at MrNathanMaths@proton.me. Parents and students are very welcome to get in touch between lessons with any questions or concerns. No question is too small.

Getting Ready for Your First Lesson

What your child needs for each lesson

What to Bring to Every Lesson

Please make sure your child has these ready at the start of each lesson:

  1. A square notepad (squared grid lines)
  2. A pencil
  3. A red pen, for corrections
  4. A ruler
  5. An eraser
  6. A scientific calculator
