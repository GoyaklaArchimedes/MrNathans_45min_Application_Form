Welcome

Welcome, and thank you for considering Mr Nathan's Maths Tuition for your child. What I offer is more than just lessons — it's a complete, structured learning partnership designed to build genuine understanding, confidence and lasting progress in maths.

Every student receives personalised one-to-one tuition tailored to their individual needs, together with their own Maths Revision Tool — included free of charge as part of the programme.

Please take a few minutes to read through the information below before clicking the Submit button at the end. I look forward to the possibility of working with you.

About the Lessons

All lessons are delivered online via Zoom and last 45 minutes. The fee per lesson is £30. Lessons are tailored to your child's individual needs, working systematically through their GCSE curriculum with a focus on genuine understanding rather than just exam technique. Every student also receives Mr Nathan's own Maths Revision Tool as part of the programme — at no extra cost.

Initial Assessment Period

The first two lessons are used to assess your child's strengths, areas for development, and learning style, and to ensure that Mr Nathan's approach is the right fit for your child. If after those first two sessions we both feel it's a good match, we just continue. There's no pressure either way — getting the right fit is what matters most.

Terms & Conditions of Tuition

45-Minute Lesson Programme

Payment

To keep everything running smoothly, payment of £30 is kindly requested at least 24 hours before each lesson. This simply ensures your child's slot is confirmed and ready to go. Payment is by bank transfer using the details on page 3. If payment has not been received by that point, the lesson will unfortunately need to be rescheduled.

Technical Requirements

A stable internet connection and a device with a working camera and microphone will ensure lessons run smoothly. It's worth doing a quick tech check before the first session!

Homework & Practice

Homework is set each week to reinforce what I've covered in the lesson, and is best done soon afterwards while everything is still fresh. It serves two important purposes — it tells me how well the material has been understood and helps me plan the direction of future lessons, and it gives your child valuable feedback on their own progress. Homework that isn't completed simply delays that feedback loop, and can mean covering the same ground again rather than moving forward. There's no pressure to be perfect — but a genuine attempt each week makes a real difference.

Commitment & Continuation

Tuition works best as a genuine team effort between student, parent, and tutor — always with the student's progress and wellbeing at heart.

Communication

All correspondence is by email. Parents and students are warmly encouraged to get in touch between lessons with any questions or concerns — no question is too small.

Getting Ready for Your First Lesson

Everything your child needs — all in one place

Step 1 — Your Child's Maths Revision Tool (TCP)

As part of Mr Nathan's Maths Tuition, every student receives their own Maths Revision Tool (TCP) free of charge. This browser-based tool is tailored to your child's GCSE level and gives them instant access to hundreds of randomised practice questions, full worked solutions, and everything needed for homework, revision and exam preparation — all in one place.

Your child's personal TCP code will be emailed to you upon confirmation of your first lesson, allowing you to download it free of charge directly from my store. The links to each tier are below so you can see exactly what your child will receive.

Which tool will my child receive?
Year 7, 8 & 9 students, and students on the Foundation tier, receive the Foundation TCP — it builds the core skills that everything else depends on. Students on Intermediate or Higher tier receive the tool that matches their current level. If your child moves to a different tier at any point, the updated tool will be provided free of charge.

Step 2 — Set Up for Writing During Lessons (Optional)

To get the most out of lessons, your child is welcome to use a pen input device to write and annotate directly on the shared whiteboard during sessions — just as they would on paper. This is entirely optional but many students find it makes lessons feel much more natural and interactive. Students who prefer not to purchase a device are encouraged to work alongside the screen using a notepad and pencil — keeping all working clearly shown, just as in an exam.

If your child uses a desktop or laptop computer

A Wacom Intuos Small drawing tablet plugs in via USB and works with any PC or Mac. No special software needed — it simply replaces the mouse for writing and annotating on screen.
